Decision Process

When stuck, walk the tree top-down: (1) identify input structure, (2) identify what the problem asks for, (3) check for keyword shortcuts. If multiple patterns seem applicable, the one that matches the most signals is usually correct. Many problems combine two patterns (e.g., Trie + Backtracking for word search on a board).
